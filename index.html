    Hamare baare mein

    FactKosh ek independent website hai jo duniya bhar ki crime news ek jagah, aasaan Hinglish mein laati hai. Har story mein hum batate hain ki ghatna kahan hui, kitne ka nuksaan hua, kitne log mare ya ghayal hue, aur police ne kisi ko pakda ya nahi.

    News roz do baar update hoti hai aur purani news date ke saath archive mein hamesha rehti hai, taaki aap kisi bhi din ki ghatna dobara dekh sako.

    Data kahan se aata hai?

    Hum public news reports padhte hain aur unka chhota summary apne shabdon mein likhte hain. Har story ke saath original source ka link diya hota hai. Summary banane mein AI ki madad li jaati hai, isliye kabhi-kabhi galti ho sakti hai — pura sach jaanne ke liye hamesha source padhein.

    Editorial policy

    Hum kisi bhi aaropi ko tab tak doshi nahi kehte jab tak court faisla na de. "Arrested" ka matlab sirf giraftaari hai, saza nahi.

    Hum peedit (victim) ki pehchaan, naabaalig bachchon ke naam ya graphic tasveerein publish nahi karte. Photos sirf us shahar ya desh ki hoti hain jahan ghatna hui, crime scene ki nahi.

    Agar kisi story mein galti dikhe, toh humein batayein — hum jaldi theek karenge.
